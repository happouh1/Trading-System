# Phase 2A review checklist

Phase 2A is an empirical-research foundation isolated from Phase 1 decision authority.

## Implemented scope

- immutable versioned experiments and append-only SQLite registry;
- deterministic expanding walk-forward folds with embargoes;
- point-in-time universe memberships including delisted fixtures;
- label-availability filtering;
- descriptive net-R and excursion statistics with seeded bootstrap intervals;
- separate calibration reports;
- training-only normalization and deterministic weighted similarity;
- append-only individual human reviews;
- canonical CSV, JSONL, Parquet, and Markdown research exports;
- architecture protection preventing decisions from importing research outputs.

## Exclusions

No parameter optimization, supervised model, decision adjustment, options logic, portfolio allocation,
brokerage, paper trading, or live connectivity is included.

## Known limitation

A production point-in-time universe vendor and revision policy remains unresolved in open question 35.
Fixture memberships validate behavior but do not support real historical-universe claims.

## Validation evidence

Record the final installation, Ruff, strict mypy, pytest, and CI results here after the implementation
commit is pushed. Phase 2A must not be tagged until all checks pass.
