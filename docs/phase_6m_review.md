# Phase 6M review

Phase 6M creates canonical content-addressed local bundles containing one verified Phase 6K
prospective-chain export and all Phase 6L assertions stored for it. The envelope separately binds
the prospective evidence chain and complete review history, retains superseded assertions, and
supports independent append-only verification.

No reviewer is authenticated, no signature or encryption is applied, no external transport is
used, and no consensus, quality threshold, promotion, production, brokerage, or live-trading
authority is introduced. Remaining trust, privacy, and distribution choices are recorded in open
questions.
